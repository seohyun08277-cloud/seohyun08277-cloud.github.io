About working

What is Mood outfit finder?

Mood Outfit Finder is a curated fashion platform that helps users explore outfits based on mood, atmosphere, and personal style. Rather than focusing solely on trends or categories, the site organizes clothing through emotional and visual narratives, allowing users to discover looks that resonate with how they want to feel.

The platform features a collection of outfits, individual items, and fashion magazine archives, creating a space where styling, inspiration, and visual culture come together.

How can I use this finder?

Users can explore the platform by navigating through different sections that present fashion from multiple perspectives.

The Outfit Archive allows users to browse curated looks based on mood and styling direction. Each outfit combines selected items to create a complete visual concept.

The Items section provides detailed information about individual pieces, including descriptions and related items, helping users understand how each element contributes to a look.

The Fashion Magazine Archive offers access to curated publications, giving additional context and inspiration drawn from fashion media.

Together, these sections allow users to discover, explore, and build outfits that reflect a specific mood or aesthetic.

What are your criteria for choosing an outfit?

Outfits are selected based on their ability to express a specific mood or atmosphere. Rather than focusing solely on trends or brands, each look is curated through a combination of silhouette, color, texture, and overall styling coherence.

The goal is to create visually balanced outfits that communicate a clear feeling whether minimal, relaxed, bold, or nostalgic while remaining wearable in everyday contexts.

Why you use random function for mood modal?

I felt that simply selecting an emotion category from the pop-up and being directed to a single, predetermined outfit would lead to a monotonous experience. So, I designed the system so that selecting a category takes users to a random outfit detail page. I saw this as one of the tools to transform the archive from a passive collection into an active one.

What is visual system of this site?

The visual system of the site is based on a clean, minimal, and editorial approach. It uses a structured grid layout to organize content clearly, allowing images and outfits to remain the primary focus.

A neutral color palette—primarily black, white, and muted tones—creates visual consistency across pages, while subtle typography and spacing maintain a calm and refined aesthetic.

The system emphasizes hierarchy, alignment, and repetition, ensuring that all sections from outfits to items and magazine archives feel cohesive and interconnected.

Why did you divide the structure?

The structure is divided to present fashion content from different perspectives while maintaining clarity and ease of navigation.

Outfit Archive, Items, and Fashion Magazine Archive serve a distinct purpose. The Outfit Archive focuses on complete looks and mood based styling, the Items section breaks down individual pieces, and the Magazine Archive provides broader visual and cultural context.

This separation allows users to explore fashion intuitively, moving from inspiration to detail without confusion.

Why choose this design style?

After reviewing various fashion websites, I noticed that they all featured clean layouts with white or black backgrounds. So, to highlight the outfits, I avoided using any other colors in the background and instead used a yellow accent color via the hover effect. In addition, thin black lines divide the expansive background and separate each outfit, allowing each one to stand out.

Is there any possibility for expansion?

If I get the chance, I'd like to collect more outfit photos and find and save items. Also, I tried to add my own outfit but couldn't figure it out, so I'd like to look into how to do it.

Mood Outfit Finder curates outfits and fashion pieces for your everyday mood.
