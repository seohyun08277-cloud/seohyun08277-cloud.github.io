About the archive

Style shaped by mood

Mood Outfit Finder is a curated fashion resource designed to connect emotion and style. This space invites viewers to explore outfits through feeling rather than trend alone, creating a more personal way of browsing fashion imagery. From calm and romantic looks to bold, playful, or effortless combinations, each outfit is part of a visual archive that reflects different moods, aesthetics, and everyday expressions of dress.

The project brings together outfit references, styling inspiration, and fashion imagery in a clean editorial format. Instead of searching by category alone, viewers can move through the archive intuitively, following atmosphere, emotion, and visual tone. The result is a resource that feels both personal and exploratory; somewhere between a moodboard, a fashion index, and a digital magazine.
